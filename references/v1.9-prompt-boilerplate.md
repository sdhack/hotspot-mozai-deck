# v1.9 默认出图提示词母版

本文件是 v1.9 视觉基线的可直接复用 Prompt 母版。凡使用当前环境图像能力生成墨仔图文时，默认以本母版组织逐页 Prompt（工具不支持参考图编辑时同样使用，仅按 prompt-template.md 的工具适配节调整）。本母版已包含中层背景封面规则与原生 3:4 画布约束；版本升级以实际成图 QA 结果为准。

## 实战适配记录（2026-09-09 验证）

- 参考图默认单人物卡一致性：仅使用 `mozai-ip-sheet-4k.jpg` 作为参考输入（拼贴故障处理见 image-generation-protocol.md）。
- 原生 3:4：请求 `size: 1024x1365` 可直接得到 3:4 成图（实际返回约 1086×1448）。优先原生 3:4；仅当工具只有 2:3 档位时才允许两侧延展留白补边，且必须复核四边留白均衡。
- 满幅构图：Prompt 中写明"边框距画布四边约 30px、构图满幅、禁止大片空白纸边"，否则模型默认留宽纸边。
- 若当前工具提供编辑端点，返回可能是 `url` 字段或 `b64_json`，解析时按当前工具实际响应兼容；URL 下载超时按当前环境设置，不固化具体秒数。
- 参考图优先用 `mozai-ip-sheet-4k.jpg`；注意在 Prompt 中写明"参考图仅定义外观，不得复刻设定卡版式/文字/多姿势/色卡"。

## CANVAS（逐页固定）

```text
True portrait 3:4 canvas. The hand-drawn double-line border sits only about 30px inside the canvas edges: the
composition fills the frame generously and outer blank paper margins stay thin and even on all four sides. No large
empty paper bands anywhere.
```

## STYLE（逐页固定）

```text
v1.9 hand-drawn journal news card, original Mozai style. Warm off-white to light cream paper with visible subtle
fiber grain; an extremely faint light-grey pencil grid covering the entire background. All linework is black
hand-drawn ink with natural uneven stroke weight: sparse cross-hatching, stippling dots and slight ink bleed; no
smooth vector lines, no plastic 3D shading, no heavy shadows. Low-saturation watercolor / crayon washes in mist
blue, peach pink, light sage green and pale lavender; color supports information, never competes with text. Inset
thin double-line hand-drawn border with slightly wobbly character; four corners carry matching low-density
vine-leaf, bow and star-dot flourishes. All visible text is Chinese handwritten style: title in hand-lettering
with MEDIUM-THIN light strokes (large size, never heavy thick brush or flying-white blobs), supporting text in casual pencil handwriting. No English, no pinyin, no
watermark.
```

## CHARACTER 锚点（逐页固定 + 当页动作）

```text
Exactly one Mozai, appearance strictly matching the reference character sheet (the reference defines appearance
only — never copy its sheet layout, text, arrows, multiple poses or color cards into the image): a plump pear/
teardrop black ink-drop body (height about 1.1-1.2x width, round heavy bottom tapering to a small point at the
top where the sprout grows — never a perfect circle, ball or egg; this proportion is identical on every page of
the set, scaling never changes it) with organic hand-drawn ink texture and visible hatching; one thin slightly
curled sprout stem with exactly two light sage-green leaves; large round eyes with visible white sclera and
subtly asymmetric pupils; a small restrained mouth; exactly two thin arms and two short legs; no blush, no
clothes. [当页动作/表情/位置。] Right hand clearly holds a pen chosen for this page's theme (calligraphy brush,
pencil, fountain pen or marker), fingers wrapped around the shaft.
```

## 封面中层背景（封面必带，见 v1.9-image-profile.md）

在 LAYOUT 中写明：标题留白区与墨仔之间加一层主题相关手绘水彩小景（场景道具、地点物件），约四成线强、明确淡于
前景、形成景深；场景内无人物无人脸；不压标题/副标题/胶囊；墨仔轮廓在浅背景前保持清晰。无稳定主题场景时才退回
纯留白。AVOID 中加入：human figures or faces inside scene props。

## AVOID 母版（逐页微调）

```text
a second character or extra limbs, wrong leaf count, body-shape drift between pages (circle/ball/egg/slim teardrop
instead of the locked pear teardrop proportion), reference-sheet collage or character-sheet text, faces or
human features inside icons or scene props, duplicated or misplaced page number, altered numbers or Chinese
characters, English or pinyin, watermark, page number outside the chosen fixed position, wide empty paper margins
at canvas edges
```

## 组装顺序

`CANVAS → STYLE → CHARACTER → LAYOUT → CONTENT → CONSTRAINTS → AVOID`，CONTENT 中的可见文字逐条引号列出并逐字
呈现；页码默认左上小圆圈、全套同位同字级。生成后按 qa-checklist 与单人物卡协议逐页验收。
